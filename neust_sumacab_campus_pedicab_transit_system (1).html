Where to Find Pedicabs

7:00 AM – 9:00 AM:

Pedicabs are lined up near the Campus Guardhouse (Gate #58) inside the university entrance.

After 9:00 AM:

Pedicabs usually wait in front of the campus outside the main university entrance.

Why It Is Convenient to Use Pedicabs Inside the Campus

Wide Campus Area: The Sumacab Campus is large and spread out. Pedicabs make it easier and faster for students and staff to travel between buildings, laboratories, sports facilities, and other areas of the campus.
Safe and Eco-Friendly: Pedicabs are powered by people and do not produce harmful emissions. They also help reduce the use of fast-moving vehicles inside the campus, making the area safer for students and staff.
Convenient During Hot or Rainy Weather: Pedicabs provide protection from the heat and rain. They are also useful for students and staff carrying heavy bags, school projects, or other materials.
Affordable Transportation: Pedicab fares are kept affordable, making them a practical and budget-friendly way to travel short distances within the campus.
Support for Local Drivers: Allowing pedicabs inside the campus provides local drivers with a source of income while giving students and staff a convenient transportation option.
